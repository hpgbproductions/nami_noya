# Lost Planet 1 Extreme Difficulty Journal

This is a collection of notes for my current attempt of beating Lost Planet: Extreme Condition on Extreme difficulty. It might serve as a guide for those who want to play the funny soulslike mech game.

This is a game that has been with me for my whole life. I started it before primary school (?) without making progress, beat the three standard difficulties as a teenager, and is now making progress on Extreme. I don't know who's playing this game in the second quarter of the 21st Century, but here we go.

## Note

Most of this text assumes that you have already beaten the Easy, Normal, or Hard difficulty. However, a few tips still apply to all difficulties.

Clear time may be under-reported as I sometimes restart from the pause menu if I haven't reached a checkpoint yet.

## General Tips

- Prior knowledge of the maps and bosses is very useful (obviously).
  - You need all the T-ENG you can get.
  - There are no data points.
- When fighting, be quick, but don't aggro too many enemies at once.
- Certain parts of missions are different on Extreme difficulty. Generally, there are:
  - More enemy spawns (including goons with the bosses).
  - Fewer unoccupied VSes.
- Your loadout (including the VS you bring in) is saved when you enter the boss arena.
  - Bring the right weapons and sufficient ammo.
  - Enter with as much T-ENG as possible.
  - The VS will be repaired.
- If your T-ENG or ammo is low, it will be restocked to a predetermined amount when respawning at a checkpoint:
  - T-ENG: 1000 units.
  - Machine gun: 300 units.
  - Hand grenade: 10 units.
  - Not sure about the behavior for other weapons.
- Try some weapons you previously ignored.

## Prologue

It's basically impossible to fail in this level. Take the chance to warm up and get a feel for how fast the T-ENG drains.

## Mission 1

### Snow Area

TAKE THE SHOTGUN!! It is very good. And keep your grenade reserves high. The rocket launcher might be good too, but I didn't use it.

### Akrid Nest

Run past all the enemies. I don't think it's possible to make it with more than 1000 T-ENG; you can abuse the respawning behavior to get more T-ENG.

### Boss

There are no Vital Suits for you to use. The VS wrecks have weapons, but don't use them, you won't be able to move when hip-firing them.

Firstly, four Dongos (rolling Akrid) will spawn on the sides as goons. After a few seconds, the boss will appear in the middle.

My strategy was to take out the two Dongos on the right. Use 1-2 grenades and rush in with the shotgun. Pray that you don't get cornered and focus on clearing the goons. The shotgun has a chance of staggering them even when not aiming at the weak spot.

Listen for the screech and initial wind-up spin that telegraphs a rolling attack. (Note that rarely, it does not wind-up.) Grenades can cancel the attack. I think any grenade splash damage will do. If the grenade touches a rolling enemy, it will explode instantly, which may be dangerous for you.

When you use the shotgun on the boss, it will drop T-ENG. It's just enough to stay alive. Hit the eggs when convenient.

### Discussion

Completed in about 50 minutes (excluding initial attempt many years ago). The exploration phase is easy. This boss is fair, I don't feel like I lose due to things completely out of my control. There is still a chance of being ganked, or having unfortunate positioning where the spin attack cannot be cancelled or avoided, but the attempts are fast and fun.

## Mission 2

### City

For the Akrid part, it's up to you whether to fight or run past.

For the Snow Pirate part, you might try to take control of a GTF-13M for a bit.

### Bridge and Tunnel

Very tough. Sniping is useful. (Did you know that the rifle has a scope?) Even if you manage to pilot snipe the GTF-13M on the bridge, don't try using it on the ramps, it's finicky.

The VS at the end of the bridge can be backed into the right-hand side corner using regular hand grenades. Alternatively, it might be easily skippable.

### Pirate Base Entrance

Use the rifle to snipe the pilots in the two GTF-11s, and quickly rush to commandeer one of them. It makes short work of the turrets and other VS further inside. Get the T-ENG and enter the base.

I think it is impossible to enter with the VS. This is unless I have skill issue.

### Boss

The main difficulty of the boss is not having a VS to fight it with. This simple change forces almost perfect gameplay.

Take the VS shotgun right in front of you and defeat the two GTF-11s. Unfortunately, I think it is impossible to steal them, I tried using the rifle and machine gun but the pilots refused to jump out. While attacking the goons, make your way to the back left or back right corner, with two VS rocket launchers or a VS shotgun respectively. With these additional supplies, you must defeat the goons and the boss GTB-22.

The boss can deploy a smokescreen when it jumps. Pay attention and always look for it! Jumping and landing both have a very small AoE. In addition, the boss has two main attacks:

Firstly, the missile launcher. The boss will jump away, usually onto the tower in the middle or the buildings on the sides, but note that it can also start the attack while on the ground. When in position, an arm- or hook-shaped part of the missile launcher will retract, and the boss will launch 1-4 missiles. This attack is an easy opening for the rocket launcher. It's basically only dangerous if you lose track of the boss.

The other attack is much more dangerous. It will do ground pounds with a large AoE. This is spammable, and if your positioning is unfortunate, you will die. You need to keep some distance to be safe, unfortunately this makes the VS shotgun less effective. You might try to camp the center tower or the garage shutters at the far end to avoid this attack, but you will have to contend with the people in the center.

There is a secret third attack, where it jumps around the other side of the arena and wastes your T-ENG.

### Discussion

Completed in about 4 hours and 20 minutes. The exploration phase requires tactical play. The boss is terrible. It is very luck-based as there is a chance of a lethal and unavoidable attack. Boss attempts are quite long.

### Boss

Not different from the standard difficulties. You get two VSes to play with. (Devs really should just give one.)

### Discussion

## Mission 4

### Mountain Route

Run past as many enemies as you can. Take some ammo for human-sized weapons, but you won't need much of it. Keep the machine gun. The missile launcher may be useful.

### Mountain Facility

Hang from the hole using the grapple, clear the floor, and run. Don't take too long as it's unlikely that you can gain T-ENG here. There is a checkpoint at the antechamber with the plasma grenades.

Snipe the pilot out of the enemy VS using the machine gun, and use it to defeat the other enemies (including another VS further inside). Make sure to get T-ENG from the large cylindrical tanks, and if you're lucky you might end up with more than 3000.

Take the GTB-22 up the platforming section. Pick up some gatling gun ammo and the rocket launcher close to the top.

There is a buried GTB-22 with a missile launcher and gatling gun before entering the arena. DON'T switch GTB-22s, it will be repaired when you retry, and you can pick up the buried one during the boss fight.

### Boss

I entered with the lower GTB-22 and less than 1000 T-ENG (resets to 1000). At the start, the boss will fly around the central pillar a little without attacking. Use this time to change your loadout and get more T-ENG.

Hitting the glowing abdomen with rockets or missiles will cause the boss to drop T-ENG; you need it to retain GTB-22 privileges. Gatling guns don't drop T-ENG, but can shoot down the boss' missiles. Laser rifles seem to do sometimes, but they consume T-ENG and don't do much damage, so they're not viable.

I tried many loadouts for the lower GTB-22, and the winning combination was dual rocket launchers (1 pickup from the platforming area + 2 pickups on the arena pillar). Shoot 2 rockets with a short delay! If you do it right, you do more damage than 2 rockets at the same time. (Maybe when two rockets are fired simultaneously, the first to hit will detonate the other, meaning you get 1 direct hit and 1 AoE only).

The main rocket opening in the first phase is the boss' missile attack, where it points its stinger towards you and then downwards, and stays still for a while as it cannot chain this attack. This opening becomes unreliable in the second phase as it gains the ability to chain that attack. You should use the boss' artillery (white projectile) and charging attacks instead. The charging attack can be avoided using the central pillar or double jumping.

In my winning run, the boss destroyed my first GTB-22 set up with dual rocket launchers, but had very little HP left. I dug up the other one and beat it with 400 T-ENG to spare.

### Discussion

Completed in about 2 hours and 40 minutes. The boss is very challenging. Attacks are finicky to avoid. I feel like it's very luck-based. Just like fighting a mosquito.
