# Lost Planet 1 Extreme Difficulty Journal

This is a collection of notes for my current attempt of beating Lost Planet: Extreme Condition on Extreme difficulty. It might serve as a guide for those who want to play the funny soulslike mech game.

This is a game that has been with me for my whole life. I started it before primary school (?) without making progress, beat the three standard difficulties as a teenager, and is now making progress on Extreme. I don't know who's playing this game in the second quarter of the 21st Century, but here we go.

## Note

Most of this text assumes that you have already beaten the Easy, Normal, or Hard difficulty. However, a few tips still apply to all difficulties.

## General Tips

- Prior knowledge of the maps and bosses is very useful (obviously).
- Certain parts of missions are different on Extreme difficulty. Generally, there are:
  - More enemy spawns (including goons with the bosses).
  - Fewer unoccupied VSes.
- Your loadout (including the VS you bring in) is saved when you enter the boss arena.
  - Bring the right weapons and sufficient ammo.
  - Enter with as much T-ENG as possible.
  - I think the VS will be repaired.
- If your T-ENG or ammo is low, it will be restocked to a predetermined amount when respawning at a checkpoint:
  - T-ENG: 1000 units.
  - Machine gun: 300 units.
  - Hand grenade: 10 units.
  - Not sure about the behavior for other weapons.
